ExpenseIQ · Turn 5 · One flow, both platforms

The whole journey, start to finish

Desktop - the entry point is a permanent composer docked at the bottom of the workspace: click the mic, or hold space from anywhere. 5a idle → 5b listening → 5c confirm → 5d logged.

Mobile - nav resolved. The tab bar is the constant chrome and its raised centre button is the mic, so the four-second flow starts from the home screen rather than a separate chromeless launch screen: 5e home → 5f listening → 5g confirm → 5h logged. The tab bar hides only while the sheet is up, so the thumb never has to move. This supersedes the chromeless 3a–3e screens below; the menu sheet (4d) covers everything a sidebar would, so no drawer is needed.
